3 Bidang Penyelidikan Utama dalam PendidikanMajor Research Areas in Education Major Research Areas in EducationBidang Penyelidikan Utama dalam Pendidikan ▼
BM
Penyelidikan pendidikan membantu guru mengenal pasti apa yang perlu diperbaiki, memahami mengapa masalah berlaku, dan menentukan bagaimana inovasi boleh dibangunkan.
EN
Educational research helps teachers identify what needs improvement, understand why a problem occurs, and determine how an innovation can be developed.
  • 1 · Penyelidikan KurikulumCurriculum Research / Curriculum Research / Penyelidikan Kurikulum Fokus: Kandungan kurikulum, piawaian pembelajaran, urutan topik dan relevansi.
    Contoh: Modul pembelajaran yang membahagikan konsep kompleks kepada aktiviti progresif.    Focus: Curriculum content, learning standards, topic sequencing and relevance.
    Example: A learning module that breaks complex concepts into progressive activities.
  • 2 · Penyelidikan PedagogiInstructional & Pedagogical Research / Instructional Research / Penyelidikan Pedagogi Fokus: Strategi pengajaran, perancah dan interaksi guru–murid.
    Contoh: Pembelajaran berasaskan projek, inkuiri atau permainan pendidikan.    Focus: Teaching strategies, scaffolding and teacher–student interactions.
    Example: Project-based learning, inquiry-based learning or educational games.
  • 3 · Penyelidikan PentaksiranAssessment & Evaluation Research / Assessment Research / Penyelidikan Pentaksiran Fokus: Pencapaian pembelajaran, maklum balas dan pentaksiran formatif.
    Contoh: Rubrik digital, kuiz diagnostik atau sistem maklum balas serta-merta.    Focus: Learning achievement, feedback and formative assessment.
    Example: Digital rubrics, diagnostic quizzes or instant feedback systems.
  • 4 · Sains PembelajaranLearning Sciences Research / Learning Sciences / Sains Pembelajaran Fokus: Cara murid memahami konsep, membina pengetahuan dan menyelesaikan masalah.
    Contoh: Aktiviti pembelajaran visual, simulasi dan latihan berperancah.    Focus: How students understand concepts, construct knowledge and solve problems.
    Example: Visual learning activities, simulations and scaffolded exercises.
  • 5 · Teknologi PendidikanEducational Technology Research / Educational Technology / Teknologi Pendidikan Fokus: Teknologi digital, AI, simulasi dan platform pembelajaran.
    Contoh: Aplikasi pembelajaran interaktif atau makmal maya.    Focus: Digital technologies, AI, simulations and learning platforms.
    Example: Interactive learning applications or virtual laboratories.
  • 6 · Pendidikan InklusifInclusive Education Research / Inclusive Education / Pendidikan Inklusif Fokus: Kebolehan, keperluan, motivasi dan sokongan pembelajaran murid.
    Contoh: Bahan pembelajaran terpilah dengan pelbagai tahap kesukaran.    Focus: Students' abilities, needs, motivation and learning support requirements.
    Example: Differentiated materials with multiple difficulty levels.
  • 7 · Pembangunan Profesional GuruTeacher Professional Development Research / TPD Research / Pembangunan Profesional Fokus: Kompetensi guru, amalan reflektif dan penambahbaikan pedagogi.
    Contoh: Kit sumber guru, PKP atau modul latihan.    Focus: Teacher competencies, reflective practice and pedagogical improvement.
    Example: Teacher resource kits, PLCs or training modules.
  • 8 · Persekitaran PembelajaranLearning Environment Research / Learning Environment / Persekitaran Pembelajaran Fokus: Penglibatan murid, pengurusan tingkah laku dan iklim bilik darjah.
    Contoh: Stesen pembelajaran, rutin kolaboratif atau sistem penglibatan.    Focus: Student engagement, behaviour management and classroom climate.
    Example: Learning stations, collaborative routines or engagement systems.

Panduan Cepat: Cabaran → Bidang PenyelidikanQuick Guide: Challenge → Research Area

Murid tidak faham konsep? → Sains Pembelajaran. Strategi pengajaran tidak berkesan? → Pedagogi. Tiada bahan/modul sesuai? → Kurikulum + R&D. Pelajar tidak terlibat? → Persekitaran Pembelajaran. Ingin guna teknologi/AI? → Teknologi Pendidikan. Pelajar pelbagai tahap? → Pendidikan Inklusif.

Students don't understand concepts? → Learning Sciences. Teaching strategies not effective? → Pedagogy. No suitable materials/modules? → Curriculum + R&D. Low student engagement? → Learning Environment. Want to use technology/AI? → Educational Technology. Mixed-ability students? → Inclusive Education.

Kit Bukti: Apa yang Dikumpul di Setiap PeringkatEvidence Toolkit: What to Collect at Each Stage

Peringkat 01–03 (Kenal Pasti): Keputusan ujian, catatan pemerhatian, temu duga murid, rekod kerja, soal selidik diagnostik. Peringkat 04 (Reka Bentuk): Ulasan pakar, ujian kesahan kandungan (CVR), semakan rakan sejawat. Peringkat 05 (Laksana): Jurnal guru, rekod kehadiran, catatan lapangan, borang penilaian formatif. Peringkat 06 (Nilai): Ujian pra-pasca, Cohen's d, peratusan penambahbaikan, temu duga pasca.

Stages 01–03 (Identify): Test results, observation notes, student interviews, work samples, diagnostic questionnaires. Stage 04 (Design): Expert review, content validity ratio (CVR), peer review. Stage 05 (Apply): Teacher journals, attendance records, field notes, formative assessment forms. Stage 06 (Evaluate): Pre-post tests, Cohen's d, improvement percentages, post interviews.

Kajian Kes 4Case Study 4
Perancah Bertingkat Dibantu AIAI-Assisted Tiered Scaffolding
Nurul H. Noordin, Sr. Member IEEE
📊 d≈1.86 n=115 p<.001 7-week RCT

Kumpulan AI mencapai keuntungan pembelajaran jauh lebih besar (d≈1.86) — cara AI diintegrasikan lebih penting daripada kehadirannya.AI group achieved far greater learning gains (d≈1.86) — how AI is integrated matters more than its mere presence.

Lihat perincianShow details ▼

Kajian kawalan rawak kaedah campuran 7 minggu (n=115): kumpulan eksperimen (n=60) menggunakan AI Generatif melalui 4 fasa Cognitive Apprenticeship; kumpulan kawalan (n=55) tanpa AI. Kedua-dua kumpulan membangunkan Permainan Slider Python yang sama. Etika: IREC2023-173; data 2023–2025.7-week mixed-methods randomised controlled trial (n=115): experimental group (n=60) used Generative AI through 4 Cognitive Apprenticeship phases; control (n=55) without AI. Both groups developed the same Python Slider Game. Ethics: IREC2023-173; data 2023–2025.

Pengajaran:Lesson: M=25.51 vs M=6.90, d≈1.86, t(113)=11.64, p<.001 — cara AI diintegrasikan melalui perancah berstruktur lebih penting daripada kehadiran AI itu sendiri.M=25.51 vs M=6.90, d≈1.86, t(113)=11.64, p<.001 — how AI is integrated through structured scaffolding matters more than its mere presence.
